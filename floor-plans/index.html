Townhouse and villa layouts compared

Every layout sits in the same masterplan of parks, shops and workplaces. The difference is how much of the house is yours alone.

Townhouse layouts

Part of a connected row. The 3-bedroom plan, about 2,040 sq. ft., lets the third room work as a study or guest room. The 4-bedroom plan, about 2,200 sq. ft., gives each child a door of their own.

Twin villa layout

Four bedrooms, built as one of a pair with a single shared wall. Each home has its own entrance and garden.

Standalone villa layouts

Three or four bedrooms with no shared walls. The most private homes in the collection, with areas released at launch.
