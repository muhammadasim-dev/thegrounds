Privacy policy

How thegrounds.ae collects and uses the details you share when you register interest in The Grounds by Aldar.

Last updated: 26 November 2026. thegrounds.ae is an independent marketing site for The Grounds by Aldar. It is not the official Aldar website.

What we collect

When you use the registration form we collect your full name, email address, phone number, whether you are a buyer or a broker, your message, and the page you sent it from. Our hosting provider also records the country your request came from.

The site uses Google Tag Manager and Google Analytics to measure visits. These tools use cookies and record pages viewed, device type and approximate location. We do not send your name, email or phone number to analytics.

Why we use it

  • To contact you about The Grounds by Aldar: launch dates, prices, floor plans, availability and the brochure.
  • To have a licensed property advisor follow up on your enquiry.
  • To understand which pages are useful, so the site can be improved.

Registering does not reserve a home and does not commit you to anything.

Who receives it

Your enquiry is stored in our customer relationship system (Salesforce) and may be sent to our team by email. Service providers that host the site or process enquiries for us handle it only on our instructions. We do not sell your details.

How long we keep it

We keep enquiry details while the project is being marketed and for as long as needed to follow up your interest, then delete or anonymise them.

Your choices

You can ask us to stop contacting you, or to see, correct or delete your details, at any time. Reply to any message we send you, or send the registration form with the word "delete" in the message. We will act on the request within 30 days.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is published on this page with a new date.
