The community

Live well, outdoors.

The Grounds brings homes, shops and workplaces together with green open space, so a day in the neighbourhood does not have to start with a long drive. Aldar is developing it with Dubai Holding in Dubai Silicon Oasis, beside Dubai International Academic City.

About 1,800 townhouses, twin villas and standalone villas give families a choice of scale. Sales are expected to open in the first week of December 2026. Completion is indicated for the second quarter of 2030.
