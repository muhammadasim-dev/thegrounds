Who it suits

A house, with less plot to keep.

The shared green around a townhouse does some of the work a private garden would do. Typical buyers are families leaving an apartment, couples who want a study, and owners who would rather live close to shops than on a large plot.

  • Families moving from an apartment to a house.
  • A third bedroom as a study or guest room.
  • A fourth bedroom when each child needs a door of their own.
  • Buyers who want the community’s parks more than a large private garden.

Ask at launch

  • Which 3- and 4-bedroom plans exist, and how the rooms are sized?
  • Which streets or clusters face the green open space?
  • What is in the specification, and what is optional?
  • What service charge is expected once the home is complete?
  • How are parking and storage arranged?

Townhouse or twin villa?

Same community. The difference is how the home sits on the ground.

Townhouse

Part of a connected row. Three or four bedrooms. The lower guide prices, from AED 2.85M, and the smaller areas.

Twin villa

Built as one of a pair. Four bedrooms, its own entrance and garden, from AED 3.95M. More separation than a row, less than a detached villa.

The Grounds townhouse questions

The figures quoted ahead of launch are about 2,040 sq. ft. for three bedrooms and about 2,200 sq. ft. for four. Treat both as approximate until the floor plans are issued.

Tenure is confirmed in the launch documents and the sale and purchase agreement. Check that agreement for the home you reserve.

Resales are part of how Dubai communities work, but the rules for The Grounds will be set out in the sales documents. Nothing on this page promises an upgrade path.

Guide prices start at AED 2.85 million for a 3-bedroom townhouse of about 2,040 sq. ft., roughly AED 1,400 per sq. ft. The 4-bedroom townhouse of about 2,200 sq. ft. starts at AED 3.5 million. The full table is on the price list page.

The plan quoted ahead of launch is 5% on booking, 45% during construction and 50% on handover. On a AED 2.85 million townhouse that is AED 142,500 to book. The payment plan page works through each home.
