How to choose

Separation is the real difference.

Every home at The Grounds shares the parks, the shops and the workplaces. Choose a villa when the household wants its own entrance, a garden and fewer neighbours through the wall.

  • Townhouse: a connected row, from AED 2.85M.
  • Twin villa: one shared wall, own front door, four bedrooms.
  • Standalone villa: no shared walls, three or four bedrooms.
