The Grounds by Aldar payment plan

The quoted payment plan for The Grounds by Aldar is 5% on booking, 45% during construction and 50% on handover. The sale agreement confirms the dates, the escrow account and any post-handover option.

What is the payment plan at The Grounds?

A 5/45/50 split is the plan currently quoted for every home type. Instalment dates inside the 45% are set in the sale agreement, not on this page.

5%

On booking

The first payment when the home is reserved. This is the amount that takes it off the market, not the enquiry form.

45%

During construction

Instalments through the build, tied to dates or milestones in the sale agreement. Completion is indicated for Q2 2030.

50%

On handover

The balance when the home is handed over. A post-handover plan has not been announced. Ask at launch if one is offered.

Beyond the price

Budget the costs around the purchase

The guide price is not the full cost of buying off-plan in Dubai. On the AED 2.85 million townhouse, the 4% Dubai Land Department fee alone is about AED 114,000. Confirm every fee in the sales documents and with a legal adviser before you reserve.

4% DLD fee

The Dubai Land Department registration fee is generally 4% of the price.

Oqood

Off-plan sales are registered on Oqood, Dubai’s off-plan register. A small fee applies.

Service charges

These start once the home is complete. The rate for The Grounds is not published yet.

Finance fees

If you use a mortgage, the lender’s fees sit outside the developer’s price. Eligibility depends on the lender and the build stage.

From booking to handover in five steps

Off-plan purchases in Dubai usually follow this shape. The Grounds uses it once the launch documents exist.

  1. 01Register interest. Free, and it reserves nothing.
  2. 02Prices, floor plans and availability at launch.
  3. 03Pay the 5% booking amount and register the sale.
  4. 04Follow the 45% construction instalments in the agreement.
  5. 05Handover, and the final 50% the agreement sets.
