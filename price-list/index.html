Reading the prices

How much does a home at The Grounds cost?

Prices at The Grounds by Aldar start at AED 2.85 million for a 3-bedroom townhouse of about 2,040 sq. ft. That works out at roughly AED 1,400 per sq. ft. Larger homes are not priced at that same rate, so the per sq. ft. figure only applies to the entry townhouse.

The collection then steps up: AED 3.5 million for a 4-bedroom townhouse, AED 3.95 million for a 4-bedroom twin villa, AED 4.1 million for a 3-bedroom standalone villa and AED 4.8 million to AED 4.9 million for a 4-bedroom standalone villa.

What moves a price

  • Home type: townhouse, twin villa or standalone villa.
  • Bedrooms: three or four.
  • Position in the masterplan, such as facing the green.
  • Plan and plot, confirmed in the launch price list.
